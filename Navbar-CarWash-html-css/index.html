The Sunset Car Wash Story

Built in our community, driven by quality, and created for a great experience.

Our Beginning

Founded in 2024, Sunset Car Wash began with a simple goal: to bring professional car care services to our community while creating local opportunities and fostering connections between customers and neighbours.

The Perfect Combination

While your car receives the premium treatment it deserves, you can relax and enjoy authentic South African braai prepared by our skilled team. It's efficiency meets enjoyment - the Sunset way.

Excellence & Innovation

From our humble beginnings, we've focused on improving our services, creating a better customer experience, and delivering quality results with very wash.
